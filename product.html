Meet Clair Alpha

Air Index Quality

Clair Alpha continuously maps the invisible elements of your environment, it synthesizes a comprehensive Air Quality Index.

Clair Specs

Core Processing
Powered by an advanced ESP32 architecture. This serves as the central intelligence hub, instantly processing multi-sensor data and ensuring seamless Wi-Fi transmission to your network.
Atmospheric Sensing
Equipped with an SCD41 module for simultaneous, laboratory-grade monitoring. It tracks CO2 saturation, ambient temperature, and relative humidity with absolute precision.
Laser Particle Detection
Utilizes a high-precision PMS5003 laser sensor to continuously measure ultra-fine particulate matter, actively tracking both PM2.5 and PM10 pollutants down to the sub-micron level.
Unobtrusive Interface
Features a minimalist high-contrast OLED display. Designed for zero distraction, it provides immediate, app-free visibility of critical metrics without compromising the device's sharp, pure silhouette.

Take absolute control of your indoor environment. Precise, real-time tracking for CO2, particulate matter, temperature, and humidity.
